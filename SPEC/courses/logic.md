### `advanced` — Advanced logic

- `adv-1` — Graphs & paths. Міста A-B-C-D у лінію. Скільки ребер у шляху A→D? (2 exercises)
- `adv-2` — Probability intuition. Монета 2 рази. Ймовірність 2 орли (приблизно)? (2 exercises)
- `adv-3` — Coding puzzles. Якщо CAT=3120, DOG=4157, то GOD=? (2 exercises)

- `adv-5` — Advanced finale. 3, 5, 9, 17, ? (4 exercises)

- `adv-7` — Conditional logic. Якщо A→B і B→C, то A→C? (2 exercises)

### `deduction` — Deduction · sets

- `ded-0` — Deduction intro. Якщо всі A — B, і X — A, то X — ? (3 exercises)
- `ded-1` — Syllogisms light. Усі коти — тварини. Мурчик — кіт. Отже Мурчик — ? (2 exercises)
- `ded-2` — Sets. A = {1,2,3}, B = {2,3,4}. A ∩ B? (3 exercises)
- `ded-3` — Odd one out. Яке не підходить: dog, cat, bird, car? (2 exercises)
- `ded-4` — If-then conditions. Якщо дощ — беру парасольку. Парасольки немає. Отже? (2 exercises)

### `multi-step-deductions` — Multi-Step Deductions

- `grid-deductions-order` — Order and positions: grid deductions. Four runners finished: A before B, C between A and B, D finished last. Who finished second? (2 exercises)
- `knights-and-knaves` — Knights and knaves logic. Knights always tell truth, knaves always lie. X says: «At least one of us (me or Y) is a knave». Who are they? (2 exercises)
- `conditional-syllogisms` — Conditional syllogisms & fallacies. Rule: «If syntax error, compiler emits error». Fact: «Compiler emitted error». Is it necessarily a syntax error? (2 exercises)
- `checkpoint-deduction-1` — Checkpoint: deduction reasoning 1. Exam. 3 boxes with incorrect labels: «Apples», «Oranges», «Mix». Drawing an apple from «Mix», what is in «Oranges»? (4 exercises)
- `set-intersections-venn` — Set intersections & Euler circles. Group of 30 students. 20 study Python, 15 study TypeScript, 10 study both. How many study neither? (2 exercises)
- `temporal-scheduling` — Task scheduling & dependency ordering. B depends on A. C depends on B. D parallel with A/B, required for E alongside C. Min critical path if each task is 1h? (2 exercises)
- `elimination-matrices` — Elimination matrices & constraint solving. Anna, Borys, Viktor: dev, designer, QA. Anna is not dev. Borys sits next to designer, not designer. Who is dev? (2 exercises)
- `cause-and-fallacy` — Cause, correlation & cognitive traps. «After new release, traffic increased 20%. Therefore the design change caused the increase». What fallacy is this? (2 exercises)
- `checkpoint-deduction-2` — Checkpoint: multi-step logic exam. Exam. Wason selection: cards [A], [B], [4], [7]. Rule: «If vowel, other side is even number». Which cards must you turn over? (4 exercises)
